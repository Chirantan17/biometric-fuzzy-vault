Why deterministic hashing algorithms fail on human biology, and why storing raw coordinates is catastrophic.

Cryptographic hashes (SHA-256) enforce strict bit diffusion. A 1-bit change flips > 50% of the output digest. Because natural biometric noise alters coordinate bits on every scan, deterministic hashing causes a 100.0% False Reject Rate (FRR).

System Critique & Unsolved Challenges

A transparent assessment of architectural limitations and future engineering directions.

1. Geometric Pre-Alignment Dependency

The standard Juels-Sudan scheme requires scanned minutiae to share an identical spatial reference frame. If a user touches the sensor at a 30° rotation, coordinate intersection collapses. Our current engine assumes pre-alignment via singular core points. Future work entails integrating Geometric Hashing for rotation invariance.

2. Cross-Vault Correlation Risks

If an individual enrolls the identical fingerprint across two independent databases protected by different polynomial secrets, an adversary possessing both public vaults can isolate genuine minutiae by calculating the set intersection of coordinate pairs. Addressing this requires non-linear coordinate transforms.
